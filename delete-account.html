Delete your AlWadiya Home account

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Tap the account icon at the top → Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and enter your password to confirm.

Option 2: without the app

Message us on WhatsApp at +970 56 645 5515 with "Account deletion request" and your registered email. We will delete the account within 7 days at most and confirm when it's done.

What gets deleted?

Your account (email and password), your name and phone number, and all your favorites. Deletion is permanent and we keep no copy of this data. General analytics data (Firebase Analytics) is not linked to your name and is deleted automatically according to Google's retention period.

Privacy Policy

AlWadiya Home — Last updated: November 18, 2026 — العربية
AlWadiya Home lists properties for sale and property requests in the Gaza Strip. You can browse the whole app as a guest without giving us any information about yourself. This page explains what data we collect when you use certain features, why, and how to delete it.

1. Data we collect

  • Browsing as a guest: we don't ask for any personal data.
  • Creating an account: your email address, password and mobile number. The password is stored encrypted by Google's Firebase Authentication service; we cannot see it.
  • Optional data: your name, only if you add it yourself on your profile page.
  • Favorites: the properties and requests you save, so they appear on any device you sign in from.
  • Profile picture: stays on your device only and is never uploaded.
  • New-property alerts (if you turn them on): your device is registered with Firebase Cloud Messaging so you can receive notifications about new properties in the region you chose. This is a technical device identifier and is not linked to your account. You can turn alerts off at any time.
  • Usage statistics: we use Firebase Analytics to collect general usage data and technical identifiers (such as device type, app version and the screens you open) to improve the app.

2. How we use data

  • To run your account and save your favorites.
  • To contact you on your mobile number about properties and requests you are interested in.
  • To send you the alerts you asked for.
  • To understand how the app is used and improve it.

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone for advertising.

3. Contacting us on WhatsApp

When you tap a WhatsApp button, WhatsApp opens on your device with a ready-made message containing the property's code and name. Nothing is sent unless you press send yourself, and any conversation there is covered by WhatsApp's privacy policy.

4. Services we rely on

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, data storage, notifications and analytics.
  • Cloudinary: hosting property photos (uploaded only by the app team; they contain no user data).
  • Google ML Kit: on-device translation of property descriptions.
  • YouTube: property videos are hosted on YouTube. Video thumbnails are loaded from YouTube, and tapping a video opens it in the YouTube app or your browser, under Google's privacy policy.

5. Security

All data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Database access rules prevent any user from seeing another user's data or favorites. Only the app's admin account can view registered users' name, email and phone number, and uses them solely to contact you about properties.

6. Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: Profile → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, name, phone number and favorites.
  • Without the app: message us on WhatsApp at the number below, mentioning your registered email, and we will delete your account within 7 days at most.
  • General analytics data is deleted automatically according to the retention period set in Google Analytics.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data about them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time and will change the "Last updated" date above when we do.
